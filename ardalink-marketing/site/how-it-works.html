How it works

Four boxes.
One product.

The whole platform in one picture. No jargon, no hand-waving — just the loop from the satellite to her phone, in four steps.

🛰️

1 · Satellite sees it

A fresh reading every 5 days. Free. Open.

🧠

2 · AI explains it

Compares to the last 11 years. Spots trouble.

📞

3 · Phone delivers it

Voice, USSD, or SMS — in her language.

👩🏾‍🌾

Move the herd, buy feed, wait it out.

Behind the loop

A small team, a few services, a lot of care.

The platform runs as three small services that talk to each other through a single secure database. Each one is built and shipped on its own. If one breaks, the others keep going. The code is open source — anyone can read it, anyone can help fix it.

📡 The watcher

Pulls fresh satellite readings, weather, and rainfall — every day, free, no key required.

🧠 The brain

Watches the ward for trouble. Decides who needs a call, and what to say. Speaks Swahili and English.

📞 The voice

Calls the herder. Bridges her voice to the brain in real time. Works on a 2G phone.

📊 The dashboard

What the operator sees. Live drought status, recent reports, who we've called today.
