How we make money

Free for herders.
Commission on what we help them buy and sell.

We earn her trust first — by giving her a free brief, a free call, and free answers. Then, when she's ready to act on what she learned, we help her find the right buyer or the right feed supplier. We take a small cut from the seller. The herder pays nothing.

Phase 1 · trust

Free, while we earn her trust.

For the first 18 months, ArdaLink is free for herders. No subscription. No paywall. Just the brief, the call, the menu, the SMS — for every herder in the pilot wards.

  • Free drought brief by SMS or USSD
  • Free voice call when the satellite sees trouble
  • Free water-point lookup
  • She shares what she sees on the ground — that data is the asset

Funded by the pilot budget & partner grants.

→
Phase 2 · marketplace

Then we help her find a buyer — or feed.

Once she's calling ArdaLink every week, the next question is natural: "okay — where can I sell my goats? Where can I buy feed?" That's where the marketplace comes in.

  • She asks ArdaLink for a buyer in a nearby town
  • ArdaLink matches her with a vetted buyer paying today's market price
  • The buyer pays a small commission (2–5%) on the sale
  • Same flow for feed sellers, vet services, livestock insurance

The herder pays nothing extra. The seller pays a small commission. ArdaLink takes the rest of the fee.

Why this works

Trust first. Then a transaction she actually wants.

Pastoralists don't trust apps. They don't trust strangers with their herd. But they will call back a number that already helped them survive a dry season. By the time we offer the marketplace, she's already relying on us.

1 · Trust before transaction

Every free brief builds the relationship. By month 18, calling ArdaLink is as natural as calling a neighbour.

2 · The right question at the right time

She doesn't ask "where can I find a feed seller?" in a vacuum. She asks it after a bad drought brief — exactly when the need is real and the urgency is high.

3 · Commission, not subscription

We never charge the herder. The seller's commission is paid on a successful match — aligned incentives, no paywalls, no friction.

4 · Data is the moat

By the time competitors arrive, we already know every ward, every herder, every seasonal pattern. That data is hard to replicate.

The path

What the next 24 months look like.

MONTH 1–3

Pilot launch — three wards

Bula Pesa, Garbatulla, Merti. Every herder in the pilot gets free access. We measure: how often she calls back, how often she answers, what she says.

MONTH 4–12

Listen, learn, fine-tune the dialects

Recording sessions in Borana, Turkana, Samburu, Somali. Voice quality goes up. Drop-out rate goes down. The brief becomes trusted.

MONTH 12–18

First marketplace matches

Vet a small set of buyers in Isiolo town and a feed supplier in Nairobi. Start matching the most engaged herders. Measure conversion.

MONTH 18–24

Scale across the county

Open the marketplace more broadly. Add livestock insurance and vet services. Grow the trusted-herder base. Reach sustainability on commission alone.
