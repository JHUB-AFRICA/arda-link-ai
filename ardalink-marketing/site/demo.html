One USSD session

Six taps from dial-tone to goodbye.

→ POST /api/ussd-callback  text=""
← 200  CON ArdaLink — Bula Pesa
                       1. Bula Pesa (drought brief)
                       2. Malisho (water points)
                       3. Ongea na AI (voice call)
                       4. Toka

→ POST /api/ussd-callback  text="1"
← 200  CON Bula Pesa brief:
                       1. Kwa Kiswahili (Swahili)
                       2. In English
                       0. Rudi / Back

→ POST /api/ussd-callback  text="1*1"
← 200  END Bula Pesa leo: 12% ya eneo limeathirika.
                       Hatari: high. Piga simu ArdaLink kwa ripoti kamili.

→ POST /api/ussd-callback  text="2"
← 200  END Malisho / Water points:
                       1. Bulla Pesa Borehole (SW, ~2km)
                       2. Ngare Mara Spring (NE, ~9km)
                       3. Kambi Garba Dam (SE, ~14km)

→ POST /api/ussd-callback  text="4"
← 200  END Asante. Kwaheri.

One voice call

When the satellite sees trouble, we call her.

We compose a brief in Swahili ("the pasture in your ward is drying up, two weeks ahead of last year"). We call her number. She picks up. She tells us what she sees — how the cows look, how far the water is. We listen. Her answers go straight into the next intelligence cycle.

The first version runs in real time, in her dialect, on her 2G phone. The whole loop takes less than two seconds.
